# Agicap

> Agicap is a European cash-flow and liquidity management platform (Lyon, France) for cash-flow forecasting, accounts payable/receivable, payments, banking connectivity, spend management and e-invoicing. Its Public API (api.agicap.com) exposes 19 REST APIs across Treasury, Business Documents, Payments, Banking, Accounts Receivable, Suppliers, Financing & Investments, Pre-accounting and E-Invoicing, secured with OAuth2 client-credentials.

## APIs
- [Financial investments (v1)](https://api.agicap.com/api-details/financial-investments-v1): Manage financial investments, monitor coupons and track financial income
- [Organization (v1)](https://api.agicap.com/api-details/organizations-v1): This API allows you to perform actions related to an organization
- [Purchase journal (v1)](https://api.agicap.com/api-details/purchase-journal-v1): Allows to export the accounted list of all purchase transactions
- [Authentication (v1)](https://api.agicap.com/api-details/auth-v1): This API allows you to generate your access token to use it with your http calls
- [Card expenses bank journal (v1)](https://api.agicap.com/api-details/card-expenses-bank-journal-v1): Allows to export the accounted list of all card expenses from Business Spend Management
- [Intragroup financing (v1)](https://api.agicap.com/api-details/intragroup-financing-v1): This API allows you to get intragroup financing information.
- [Treasury Bank Journal (v1)](https://api.agicap.com/api-details/treasury-bank-journal-v1): Export bank journal entries prepared in our Treasury product.
- [Webhooks (v1)](https://api.agicap.com/api-details/events-v1): This API allows you to manage webhooks to react on multiple events raised by Agicap
- [Banking Documents (v1)](https://api.agicap.com/api-details/banking-documents-v1): Import bank files into Agicap, or export the bank files retrieved by Agicap into your ERP
- [Business Documents (v1)](https://api.agicap.com/api-details/business-documents-v1): Create, retrieve, or update invoices, credit notes, sales orders, and other business documents used by Agicap
- [Business Documents (v2)](https://api.agicap.com/api-details/business-documents-v2): Create, retrieve, or update invoices, credit notes, sales orders, and other business documents used by Agicap
- [Purchase-to-Pay (v1)](https://api.agicap.com/api-details/invoices-management-v1): Use APIs to export supplier documents or update them in Agicap from an external source.
- [Suppliers (v1)](https://api.agicap.com/api-details/suppliers-v1): Synchronise your suppliers from your ERP or accounting tool into Agicap.
- [Tests (v1)](https://api.agicap.com/api-details/httpbin-v1): This API allows you to test and validate your http calls to our Public API (authentication, headers, content etc...) throught the HttpBin service default implementation
- [Client Risk (v1)](https://api.agicap.com/api-details/client-risk-v1): Set credit limits on Accounts Receivable clients.
- [Payments (v2)](https://api.agicap.com/api-details/payments-v2): Import payment files or manage beneficiaries from your ERP or accounting tool into Agicap.
- [Chart of accounts (v1)](https://api.agicap.com/api-details/chart-of-accounts-v1): Create new accounting accounts and third parties to use in Treasury's bank journal & Account Receivable's sales journal preaccounting features (does not work for Account Payable's purchase journal).
- [Clients AR (v1)](https://api.agicap.com/api-details/ar-clients-v1): Manage and synchronize clients data within the Accounts Receivable module.
- [E-Invoicing (v1)](https://api.agicap.com/api-details/einvoicing-v1): Manage e-invoicing flows
